Queens.gd is a logic puzzle game inspired by LinkedIn's Queens puzzle.

Your objective is to place a crown in each region on the board.
Every puzzle has one unique solution that is guaranteed to be solvable.

<big>Rules:</big>
<ol>
  <li>One crown per row and column</li>
  <li>One crown per region</li>
  <li>No adjacent crowns (not even diagonally)</li>
</ol>

Select from 5x5, 6x6, 7x7, or 9x9 grids. Puzzles are randomly generated on-device.
No internet connection required.
